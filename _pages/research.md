# Research

My research draws on political psychology to study political attitudes and behaviour, with a focus on political and social tolerance, attitudes towards climate change, and political polarisation.

## Political and social tolerance

I study attitudes towards controversial groups, prejudice, and the boundaries of political tolerance. My recent conference work examines political tolerance for controversial and hate-speech groups and the effects of climate change.

## Climate change and political behaviour

My recent work focuses on climate change threats and their relationship with political intolerance and authoritarian aggression. This includes survey experiments in Germany and research using the US General Social Survey.

## Social science methodology and political philosophy

My methodological interests include quantitative research methods, survey research and experiments, structural equation modelling (SEM), and qualitative comparative analysis (QCA). My published work addresses panel conditioning, intergroup contact, attitudes towards migrants, and identification with populist parties. I am also interested in political philosophy.
